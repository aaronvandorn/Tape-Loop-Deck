Matrix

Send any channel into any other, or back into itself. Sends leave after the fader and enter the destination ahead of its gain, EQ and effects, so feedback runs through the whole strip. Each return has its own delay, and a soft limiter keeps runaway loops from clipping.
MIDI map

Each strip has its own MIDI channel. Picking a channel lays out a default set of CCs (Volume 7, Balance 10, Gain 20, High 21, Mid 22, Mid freq 23, Low 24, Drive 25, Echo 26, Echo time 27, Feedback 28, Reverb 29, Tape speed 30, Mute 31, Solo 32). Change any row here or use MIDI learn.
